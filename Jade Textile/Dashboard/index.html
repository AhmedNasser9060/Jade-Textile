ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

How the metrics are calculated

Labor productivityTotal good units ÷ total paid hours
Workforce efficiencyStandard earned hours ÷ paid hours × 100
Defect rateRejected units ÷ total produced units × 100
Target attainmentActual good units ÷ planned units × 100

This dashboard uses a deterministic synthetic dataset modeled on a medium-sized garment factory. It is designed for portfolio demonstration and contains no confidential company data.
