01 The idea

One mobility platform.
Another meaningful job.

The robot was not invented for a portfolio brief. It began as a real response to a human problem: moving medicine to patients while limiting unnecessary exposure.

In a garment factory, movement is still the core challenge—only the payload changes. Instead of medicine, a mobile assistant can carry cut panels, work-in-progress bundles, or finished pieces between production stages.
